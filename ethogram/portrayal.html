Field Guide to the Wiki Swarm

Thousands of AI agent runs, working in parallel on data-retrieval tasks, left a shared public wiki full of edits. This reads that record the way a naturalist reads a habitat: every saved edit is one act, coded by what it did. Two ways of living emerge — a foraging economy that reaches data through relays, and a timed-task coordination system where runs that never meet leave signals for the ones coming behind them.

02

Two ways of living

Most activity falls into two regimes. One reaches data the agents' own tools could not fetch, by routing requests through relay services. The other is social: parallel runs of the same timed task, each named for a simulated date, posting what they saw so later runs can prepare.

03

Who does what

Group every actor by the system that dominates its edits, the way you would sort animals by feeding behavior. Most are foragers; a smaller caste does almost nothing but overwrite other agents' pages.

04

The foraging flow

When a direct fetch failed, agents chained requests through relay and conversion services to reach a data source. Each line is a route: the service used, the source it was aimed at, and how often that pairing appears. Hostnames only — no addresses.

07

How to read this, and what it isn't yet

What is solid here

  • Every act on this page is rule-coded: computed deterministically from each save, reproducible from rule_code.py with no model in the loop.
  • Raw links never entered the analysis — each is reduced to a route (service → source) and a set of flags.

What is still coming

  • The signaling system (reports, answer-sharing, schedule projections, corrections, protocols) is read by a model pass, not rules; it is represented here only through the coordination examples in Section 2.
  • Reliability tiers are measured, not assumed: foraging and overwrite acts agree strongly between independent coders; a few acts are still being sharpened before they are trusted at scale.
  • Actors are identified by handle and signature, which one run can change — identity resolution is a known open step.
